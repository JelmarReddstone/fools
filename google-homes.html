How it works

Everything Google knows, working for you

Google Homes brings together five powerful data layers to surface the right property at the right moment.

📍
Maps Intelligence
Property boundaries, walkability, school proximity, transit scores, and neighborhood character — all sourced from Google Maps and updated in real time.
🔍
Search Behavior
Anonymized intent signals from your Search history understand what you actually want — not just what you type. Nearby restaurants, gyms, commute routes — it all shapes your results.
✨
Gemini AI Matching
Our large language model cross-references your lifestyle signals with listing data to score every property on fit — delivering a personalised match percentage for every aanbod pagina.
Real-time listings
beschikbaarheidsmodule

Never miss an opening, anywhere in your market

Our AI-driven beschikbaarheidsmodule (availability module) monitors listing status across every connected market in real time — alerting you the moment a matched property becomes available, often within minutes of it leaving the agent's system.

  • Availability alerts in under 5 minutes of live listing
  • Tracks price changes, status updates & open house schedules
  • Monitors 18M+ properties across connected markets
  • Push, email, and in-app notification channels

Your personalized feed
aanbod pagina

A listings page built around your life

Your aanbod pagina (listings page) is not a generic search results page — it is a curated, AI-personalized feed that adapts to your commute, your lifestyle, and your search behavior. Every listing is scored, ranked, and explained.

  • AI match score on every listing — with reasons why
  • Ranked by commute time, neighborhood fit & price trajectory
  • Dutch & English property descriptions via Gemini translation
  • Integrated with Google Calendar for open house scheduling
  • Saved searches sync across all Google devices
Smart filtering
filters

Filters that understand your full picture

Standard filters tell you about the house. Google Homes filters tell you about your life. Layer commute intelligence, neighborhood trajectory, school ratings, and AI quality-of-life scoring on top of standard property attributes.

  • Commute time filter — by driving, cycling, or transit
  • Neighborhood trajectory score (gentrification index)
  • School rating within catchment area (Scholen op de kaart)
  • Predicted price appreciation (3-year AI model)
  • Noise map, air quality & green space index
